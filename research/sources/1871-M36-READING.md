## What the leaf is

Marriage, Biała, **May 1871** (opening: *Состоялось въ Уездномъ городѣ Бялѣ* + May *тысяча восемьсотъ семьдесятъ перваго*). Groom **18**, bachelor (*холостымъ*). Same generation as Khuna (~1853), **not** a candidate father.

Rabbi at the foot: **В. Борнштейнъ** (Wolf Bornstein). Latin signatures include **Jacob … Herzberg** and **Dawid Herzberg**. **Khuna / Хуна is not in the margin, not in the opening, and not in the signature block.**
