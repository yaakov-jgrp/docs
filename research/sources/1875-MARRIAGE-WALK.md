## Lock — M27 Иосель Гершбергъ (scan 95)

Two independent parentage crops of scan 95 agree:

- Groom **Иосель Гершбергъ**, bachelor, **19**, of Biała. Same generation as Khuna (~1853).
- Named **сынъ** of **Бенек-class × (Malka / Laika — given name not locked)** spouses **Гершбергъ**, living in Biała.
- Bride a Lenczner/Lichter-class maiden, daughter of deceased Иосель × Хая. Bride’s given name **not locked** (JRI: Chana Lenczner).
- Banns in the Biała synagogue **16 / 23 / 30 August**.
- **Khuna is not named.** This is the known JRI 1875 M27. It does **not** make Benek × Malka Khuna’s parents.
