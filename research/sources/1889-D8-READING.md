## What this does to the 1856 Dwora identity

1856 D9: **Jankiel Wajntal**, 32, son of **Josel × Fraindla**, survived by wife **Dwora, 31**. Dwora’s *own* parents are **not** on D9.

1889 D8: a **widow** named **Dwojra Wajntal**, daughter of **Jojna × Liba**. No husband, no age.

**Do not merge** these two women on this page. Compatible if 1856 Dwora’s parents were Jojna × Liba and she was still a widow in 1889; **not proved**, because D8 never says “widow of Jankiel” and the age is unpublished. JRI exact WAJNTAL + given Jojna = **none**.

This is **Liba Enta’s natal-family collateral**, not Khuna’s father or mother.
