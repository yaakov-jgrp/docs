# Garage Playroom / Homework Room — Space Planning

**Interior footprint:** 19'-0" front-to-back × 10'-4.8" left-to-right (228" × 124.8")  
**Shape:** True rectangle — no jogs, recesses, or stepped walls  
**Ceiling:** Keep existing height; flush/canless lighting only (no dropped ceiling)

## Fixed geometry (do not invent)

| Element | Location | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Front wall | Former garage door | New finished wall with several windows; meter/service equipment at **front-left** must stay accessible |
| Stair passage | **Front-right**, along right wall | **83" wide open passage** — no door; keep unobstructed; right wall stays one straight plane |
| Rear door | **Centered** on 10.4' back wall | To wash/utility; keep door + approach clear |
| Floor hatch | Water-main in slab | Same LVP finish visually continuous; **removable service hatch** (not a rusty plate) |

**Orientation used on all plans**

- Top of drawing = **FRONT** (windows)
- Bottom = **BACK** (washroom door)
- Right = stair opening
- Left = solid concrete wall

---

## Storage targets

- Books ≈ **three large/double-depth Billy-type units** → ~90–95" of tall book run, or ~45–55 linear feet of shelf
- Closed storage for toys, LEGO, games, school/craft supplies
- Prefer: closed lowers, drawers/bins, adjustable shelves, shallow vertical storage
- Avoid: deep full-height cabinets on **both** 19' walls

## Concept summaries

### Concept 1 — Front Desk / Mid Lounge / Back Library · **$$**

**Idea:** Classic three-zone strip: study at the bright front, lounge/play in the middle, tall library on the short back wall.

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Desk | 60"W × 24"D along front wall (clears 42×36 meter zone); seats **2** |
| Sofa | 84"L × 36"D along left wall |
| Books | 15"D × ~46"W each side of rear door ≈ **3.0 Billy** (~46 LF) |
| Toy storage | 18"D low cabinets on left (front & rear of sofa) |
| Shallow wall | 11–14"D shelves mid-right (adult eye level) |
| Play | ≈ **4.8' × 8.3'** central strip |
| Rolling table | 28" optional in play zone |

**Widens?** Yes — tall mass only on the short back wall; long walls stay mostly low/sofa.  
**Weakness:** Play area stays elongated; desk is tight for a third child.  
**Budget:** IKEA desk + Billy/Oxberg rear + Bestå-style lows.

### Concept 2 — Crosswise Sofa Divider · **$$** ⭐ recommended

**Idea:** Turn the sofa **parallel to the short walls** so it cuts the bowling-alley axis. Front becomes a wide play room; rear becomes study/storage.

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Window bench | 50"W × 18"D storage bench under front windows (right of meters) |
| Front play | ≈ **7.2' × 5.8'** |
| Sofa | **78"W × 36"D** crosswise; faces front windows; **~41" clear path** on right for stairs ↔ room ↔ washroom |
| Desk | 60"L × 24"D along left wall in rear; seats **2–3** |
| Pull-out | 18"D project/LEGO surface at desk end |
| Books | Rear 15"D flanks (~92") + 11"D × 68" right-rear ≈ **3.5 Billy** (~54 LF) |
| Rear nook | ~4' × 4' reading/puzzle |

**Widens?** **Strongest yes** — cross-axis sofa + crosswise rug at front.  
**Weakness:** Two smaller play zones instead of one giant rectangle.  
**Budget:** Stock sofa + IKEA desk/Billy + simple window bench box.

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|

### Concept 4 — Right Homework Gallery + Front Window Seat · **$$**

**Idea:** Long desk run on the right wall **after** the stair opening; lounge/play toward front-left; maximum homework capacity.

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Window seat | 48"W × 18"D at front |
| Sofa | 84"L × 36"D along left |

### Concept 5 — Open Mid + Back Storage Headboard · **$** ⭐ recommended

**Idea:** Play-first. Almost all tall/closed storage is a deeper “headboard” on the short back wall that also boxes pipes neatly with access panels.

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Sofa | 78"L × 36"D left-front |
| Poufs | 2 lightweight extras near front |
| Desk | 72"L × 24"D mid-left; seats **2–3** |
| Rolling table | In play field |
| Storage headboard | **18–22"D** tall units both sides of rear door (books 2-deep / bins / games) ≈ **3.0+ Billy** |
| Shallow right | 10"D display shelves only (not full-height cabinets) |
| Play | **Largest** ≈ **5.2' × 10'** |

**Widens?** Yes — storage headboard stops the eye on the short wall; long walls stay open.  
**Strength:** Lowest cost, biggest play, best utility-boxing depth.  
**Weakness:** Less built-in bench seating than Concept 3.

## Recommendation

| Priority | Pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Best overall balance | **Concept 2** | Strongest anti-corridor move; real 2–3 seat desk; clear stair path; enough books |
| Best clutter + DIY | **Concept 3** | Banquette storage; cross-room sofa; cheap closed lows |
| Best play / lowest $ | **Concept 5** | Biggest floor play; deep back wall solves books + pipes |

**Skip as primary:** Concept 4 if play openness matters more than a mega desk (keep its pull-out/pegboard ideas). Concept 1 is a solid fallback if you want the desk in the sunniest spot.

## Shared finish package (all concepts)

- **Floor:** Inexpensive LVP, continuous look over removable water-main hatch (flush framed access panel)
- **Walls:** Drywall furr-outs only where needed for wiring/boxing; paint warm light (avoid purple/gray office tones)
- **Lighting:** Flush/canless LED discs or surface slim LEDs + window light; no grid drop ceiling
- **Windows (front):** Multiple operable units for light/ventilation; leave meter clearances per code/utility
- **Storage brand mix:** IKEA Billy/Oxberg/Bestå/PAX frames, plywood tops, slab doors
- **Ceiling utilities:** Box selectively with hinged/removable panels; prefer integrating into back headboard (C5) or high shallow shelves

## Circulation rules (all concepts)

1. **Front-left 42″ × 36″** meter/service keep-clear (furniture may approach but not permanently block access).
2. **Front-right floor** must allow easy entry through the **83″** stair opening; do not clog with deep cabinets.
3. **Centered rear door** approach ≥36″ clear; storage may flank both sides.
4. Floor hatch remains **removable**; finished LVP field continues visually across it.
